About MGIT

Mahatma Gandhi Institute of Technology (MGIT), established in 1997, is a premier engineering institution in Hyderabad, Telangana, committed to fostering innovation, research, and academic excellence. Affiliated to JNTU Hyderabad and approved by AICTE, MGIT is renowned for its rigorous curriculum, world-class infrastructure, and strong industry connections that ensure exceptional career opportunities for students.

Key Highlights

  • Legacy of Excellence: Over 25+ years of shaping future engineers and leaders.
  • Campus: A sprawling 30+ acre eco-friendly campus with modern labs, libraries, and sports facilities.
  • Placements: Consistently achieves 80%+ placement rates with top recruiters like Microsoft, Amazon, and TCS.
  • Accreditations: AICTE-approved and NAAC-accredited.

Academic Strengths

Offers B.Tech, M.Tech, and Ph.D. programs in disciplines like CSE, ECE, and Mechanical Engineering. The institute emphasizes industry-aligned learning through partnerships with tech giants for workshops and internships, alongside cutting-edge research in AI and Robotics.

Student Life

Vibrant campus life with technical clubs (IEEE, CSI), an Entrepreneurship Cell, and national events like TechSrijan. The institute also promotes social responsibility through NGO collaborations.
