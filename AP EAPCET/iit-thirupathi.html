About IIT Bombay
IIT Bombay is one of India's premier engineering institutions, known for its academic excellence, cutting-edge research, and vibrant campus life. Established as the second IIT in India, it has consistently ranked among the top engineering colleges in the country.
history_edu History
Founded in 1958 with assistance from UNESCO and the Soviet Union, IIT Bombay was established to meet India's growing need for technical manpower. The institute has since grown into a world-class center for education and research in engineering and technology.
school Academics
IIT Bombay offers undergraduate, postgraduate and doctoral programs in various engineering, science, design, and management disciplines. The institute follows a semester system with a strong emphasis on research and practical learning.
groups Student Life
The campus boasts a vibrant student community with numerous clubs, societies, and annual events like Mood Indigo (cultural fest) and Techfest (Asia's largest science and technology festival). The institute has a strong tradition of student self-governance.

how_to_reg Admission Process

Admissions are based on JEE Advanced rank, followed by JoSAA counseling. Candidates must qualify JEE Main to appear for JEE Advanced.
grading Postgraduate (M.Tech)
Admissions are based on GATE score followed by written test and/or interview conducted by respective departments.
business_center MBA Program
Admissions require a valid CAT score followed by group discussion and personal interview.
